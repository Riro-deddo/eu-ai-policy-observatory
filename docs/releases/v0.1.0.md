# Dataset release v0.1.0

This is a versioned research-infrastructure snapshot, not a declaration that every EU AI document has been collected or that all annotations are complete.

## Citation

Hao, Yichen (2026). *EU AI Policy Observatory* (Version 0.1.0) [Data set]. GitHub. https://github.com/Riro-deddo/eu-ai-policy-observatory/releases/tag/v0.1.0

Use the version tag and the full source commit in the attached release manifest when recording the dataset used for an analysis. The live website and its download may change after this release. This release does not assign a DOI or introduce a new licence; source documents retain their applicable rights and terms.

## Boundaries and counts

- Coverage cutoff: **4 September 2026**. Later retrieval and review do not move this boundary.
- Release-time bounded evidence review: **6 September 2026**.
- **187 published document records**: 97 principal, 36 supporting, 30 version and 24 attachment records.
- **183 expanded-evidence verified records; 4 qualified published records remain pending**.
- Inventory: **187 included, 18 merged, 10 excluded and 12 pending candidates**.
- English manifestations are represented. Record totals are not totals of unique laws or independent analytical observations.

## What this review changed

The [retained-record ledger](../../research/reviews/2026-09-06-release-closure/retained-records.json) closes a bounded recheck of the four qualified published records. It preserves the publication-date and exact-version gaps rather than overwriting previous assessments.

The [candidate ledger](../../research/reviews/2026-09-06-release-closure/candidate-decisions.json) records successful recovery of all twelve candidate PDFs. Three AI Board reports have month-only covers and administrative registration stamps; meeting minutes do not identify the exact approved versions. Nine Parliament amendment compilations have 13 June 2022 PDF covers, while the official catalogue and HTML entries give 10 June 2022. The different date meanings may be legitimate, but the exact manifestation/publication correspondence is not established by the retrieved evidence.

All twelve remain unpublished under the unchanged evidence rules. This is a completed bounded review with unresolved findings, not twelve completed admissions. Previous candidate decisions are preserved in the inventory history. No canonical published record, relationship or past evidence-review actor is changed by this release.

## Research limitations

The corpus is bounded and expanding, not exhaustive. Some legacy discovery narratives do not retain reproducible query/result logs. Missing concept, policy or relationship annotations are not negative findings. Concept labels are discovery aids, not a completed passage-level coding study. Public reviewer credit does not create a separately timestamped human sign-off. See the repository methodology and data dictionary before constructing an analytical sample.

## Files and reproducibility

The release attaches `public-data.json`, `eu-ai-policy-observatory.sqlite`, `release-manifest.json` and `SHA256SUMS.txt`. GitHub's source archive at the version tag contains canonical records, schema, code, inventory and review ledgers. The release does not redistribute the recovered official full-text PDFs.

The manifest identifies the full Git commit, data cutoff, build timestamp, runtime versions and byte hashes. Rebuild from that commit with:

```sh
python -m pip install -e '.[test]'
observatory-build --project-root . --timestamp 2026-09-06T21:42:50Z
python -m pytest -q
```

Use `SHA256SUMS.txt` to check the downloaded release files. The hash of a release asset verifies those exact bytes. SQLite binaries generated by different Python/SQLite platforms can differ even when all table schemas and rows agree; cross-platform reproducibility must therefore compare their semantic content, not require identical SQLite byte layout.

This release's snapshot files are retained separately from later website deployments. Evidence holds should be reopened when the specific evidence described in the ledgers appears, not merely to force a zero-pending counter.
