## What improved since discovery

The original pass had 26 candidates with relevant primary text read and 8 with weaker access. Five of those eight now have primary-text evidence: DOSES, ESPRIT IV, Digitising European Industry and both EBA reports. Thus **31 of 34 now have relevant primary passages reviewed**, while three still rely on supporting text, a summary or a catalogue.

Primary-text access and admission readiness are different tests. Six of the 31 primary-text-read candidates remain held on publication-date or manifestation questions. Of the original 26 primary-text candidates, 20 are evidence ready and 6 held; of the original 8 access-limited candidates, 5 are ready and 3 held.

Final QA changed the preliminary recommendations: two Commission communications gained independent dated publication evidence, while three ESMA reports were held because their issue dates had initially been conflated with publication dates. The final counts above supersede the preliminary review counts.

- **ESMA AI in EU securities markets:** Issue date is known; exact publication date is unresolved. The 22 February 2023 newsletter establishes availability by that date only.
- **2024 CARD public report:** The dated release links the current public PDF, but the publication date of that exact public manifestation is unresolved.
- **ESMA AI in EU investment funds:** Issue date is known; the undated catalogue and file path do not establish the exact publication date.
- **ESMA AI adoption and trends in securities markets:** Issue date is known; the undated catalogue and file path do not establish the exact publication date.

The date gate does not require two different calendar days or proof of the earliest-ever release. It requires evidence attributing a publication date to the cited manifestation. A dated official presentation can support the same day as the document issue date; a PDF issue date, a file-directory month or a later catalogue appearance cannot silently substitute for that evidence.

## Material metadata and classification corrections

- **Attribution:** the EU-OSHA worker-management report has six named external authors and an agency commissioner. The Nevejans robotics study has an external author, a requesting committee and a commissioning/publishing department. Neither official hosting nor commissioning establishes institutional authorship.
- **Research versus official position:** the EPRS borders analysis and externally commissioned studies are not adopted Parliament positions. The 2021 EIOPA report is credited to its Consultative Expert Group, not corporate EIOPA.
- **Historical institutional names:** period-correct Community names are retained. Authored-origin tags for the seven decisions rely on explicit EUR-Lex Author metadata, with modern catalogue labels distinguished from historical issuing names; adoption alone is not the evidence.
- **Versions:** EASA Roadmap 2.0 explicitly updates Roadmap 1.0. The ESMA reports have different references/scopes rather than being interchangeable revisions. The 2025 AI Observatory activity-year label remains distinct from its 2026 issue and publication dates.
- **Dates and planned work:** the EMA reflection paper's issue date and separate CHMP/CVMP adoptions are retained. The NDSG workplan uses its verified publication-day fallback and month-level adoption; planned deliverables through 2028 are not past events.
- **Contract alignment, 5 September 2026 at 06:51 UTC:** seven historical Decisions now use `official_act_date`, matching their retained source date labels and specification section 5.1. The earlier ledger consolidation had incorrectly called that kind `institutional_adoption`; date values and the 25-ready/9-held decisions are unchanged.
- **Sector tags:** tags are supported by relevant passages, not inferred from a broad title. The December 2017 European Council AI-skills measure retains education only; occupational health in the worker-management report does not automatically imply the healthcare sector.
- **Relevance:** a dedicated substantive AI passage can qualify even in a broader document. Precursor is used for the reviewed expert-system/cognitive/learning provisions where explicit AI framing was not verified. The CPS study's provisional class is corrected to direct AI, but its main-text hold remains.
- **Validity:** programme completion does not prove legal expiry. Official validity statements are preserved separately from funding periods; no new current-validity assertion is manufactured from a programme end year.

These classifications are researcher recommendations, not official EU metadata. Multi-tag sector counts must never be added as though they counted unique documents.

## Why this is not imported yet

The approved historical design is not yet implemented across the data pipeline. The admission package must not lose information to fit the current contract.

1. The inventory still restricts years to 2018-2026.
2. Canonical records do not yet implement the approved temporal collection, relevance and document-date-kind fields.
3. Commissioner and bibliographic-author semantics are not yet implemented through schema, validation, SQLite, export and UI.
4. The approved historical document-type and validity distinctions still require implementation.
5. New bounded source scopes, reviewed canonical links and identity checks must accompany any later import.
6. The recorded release-verification gate remains open. This research review neither reruns nor resolves it.

The next engineering step is the approved **Phase B data-contract work**, followed by a controlled import review for the evidence-ready candidates. Held candidates must remain pending until their specific gaps are resolved. No bulk import, merge, push, deployment or live-site update was performed.
