## Reading the evidence

- **Primary text:** relevant passages were read in an opened official HTML text or primary PDF. This does not certify every page, date, author role or proposed tag.
- **Indexed text only:** official-domain indexed passages were available, but the primary text could not be opened.
- **Annex / briefing only:** supporting official text was accessible, but not the main study.
- **Official summary only:** an institutional summary supports discovery, not full-opinion verification.
- **Catalogue only:** document existence is listed officially; contents and credits remain unverified.

All metadata, locators, source URLs, role distinctions, proposed tags and outstanding checks are in the [candidate JSON](../discovery/2026-09-05-document-gap-candidates.json). These private notes are deliberately separate from the canonical inventory.

## Date, version and attribution safeguards

- The 2017 robotics and big-data resolutions were reproduced in the OJ in 2018. Each remains one historical document; the later OJ manifestation is not a second contemporary record.
- EASA Roadmaps 1.0 and 2.0 are separately citable versions. Translation, PDF and HTML manifestations are not additional versions.
- EMA's 2024 reflection paper records different CHMP and CVMP adoption dates. Both are retained; one must not silently replace the other.
- The title “2025 AI Observatory report” refers to the reporting year; the reviewed edition was issued and published in 2026.
- The NDSG 2026–2028 workplan is already published, but its future activities are not completed events.
- A 2023 hosted-file label on the EIOPA expert-group report does not by itself establish a new 2023 edition.
- Unknown publication dates remain null, including the exact first availability of the accessible CARD public PDF. Adoption, issue, OJ publication and retrieval are not interchangeable.
- Parliament research-service analysis is distinct from an adopted Parliament position. The EIOPA expert-group report is distinct from an EIOPA corporate opinion. External commissioned studies are not institution-authored merely because the EU hosts them.
- EESC is an EU advisory body; its proposed author-origin tag is `eu_agency_or_body_authored`. Historical institutional names are preserved for European Communities acts.
- Full-text access does not constitute a retained PDF archive. No local official-document snapshots or PDF checksums were created.

These distinctions were a material result of parallel institutional/sector research and PDF metadata checking, not changes to the canonical schema.
