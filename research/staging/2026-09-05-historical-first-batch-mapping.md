## Named blockers before canonical admission

- **B1 — document type:** `record.schema.json` lacks `conclusions`; add it or approve a lossless replacement. No current type is semantically equivalent.
- **B2 — legal validity:** `legal_status` has no `no_longer_in_force`/`expired` value. Do not coerce the six affected decisions to `superseded` without evidence. “Act changed” and consolidation dates also need separate preservation.
- **B3 — attribution roles:** the base schema lacks `commissioner` and `official_host`; both are present only in the inactive extension. Neither schema supports `responsible_body`, `requester`, `supervisor`, or `cover_note_sender`. Add exact roles or a richer attribution object; do not silently map them to `author` or generic `contributor`.
- **B4 — historical date shape:** the extension's `additional_dates` disallows the staged kinds `official_end_of_validity`, `official_dispatch`, and `parliament_adopted_text_manifestation`; the two Parliament records use `date` where the extension requires `value`; most staged additional dates omit required `precision`; and the DOSES `note` is rejected by `additionalProperties: false`. Reconcile the contract before transformation. Its required `date_evidence` citations must point to registered source IDs and locators, not merely copy bare dates.
- **B5 — hosts are not automatically institutions:** the review explicitly says attribution facts do not automatically define institution entities. Keep EUR-Lex, Consilium, and Parliament RegData/Think Tank represented by source records/publisher metadata unless an explicit host-entity model is approved. This also avoids fabricating institutional authorship.
- **B6 — inactive extension and year gate:** `temporal_collection`, `relevance_class`, `document_date_kind`, evidence objects, bibliographic authors, and historical roles are not accepted by the current canonical document contract; the recorded pre-2018 inventory gate also remains unresolved. Complete the chosen compatibility transition before importing any row.

The last two labels are serviceable but broader than their current names imply; record the exact publisher and URL. No source registry entries, snapshots, or hashes are created by this handoff.
