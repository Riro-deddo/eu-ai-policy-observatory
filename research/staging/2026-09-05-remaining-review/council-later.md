## Evidence boundary and access limitations

The Council Public Register results exposed current `Public` status, document date, originator/addressee and sometimes a meeting date. No checked result exposed an independently labelled publication date. Those fields were therefore not converted into `publication_date`.

Coreper related-document pages are dynamic and include materials added after the meeting. A displayed document date on such a current page was not treated as proof that the exact link was publicly present on that date.

A dated official Council announcement was accepted only when it directly linked the exact identified manifestation. This supports the INIT publication dates of 21 May 2024 and 29 June 2026 and the ST 5662 publication date of 2 February 2024. It does not turn the INIT cover date into an act date. The REV 1 records instead use their exact EUR-Lex/OJ identities and remain distinct signed manifestations.

No official dated announcement or register publication field was found for the exact ST 9645, ST 10752 or ST 10599 manifestations in this bounded second pass. Those six records remain held with explicit attribution corrections rather than being graduated on current availability or issue dates.
