## Finding

**P2 — preserve the separately evidenced AI Watch publication date with its source, not only in an uncited research note.** `data/documents/jrc-ai-watch-defining-ai-2020.json` currently has `additional_dates: []` and declares only the JRC PDF/repository sources. Its researcher note mentions 21 February 2020, but the inspected AI Watch source explicitly labels that date **Publication date** (`candidate-jrc118163-aiwatch.html`, line 603). The approved historical-scope specification section 5.1 requires a separately evidenced earlier official publication to remain an additional explicitly labelled date. Keep 27 February as the cited JRC repository manifestation's `publication_date` and publication fallback; add the AI Watch source and a separately evidenced `first_official_publication` entry for 21 February, with the existing qualification that the current AI Watch download has a later 2022 link stamp. This corrects preservation and traceability; it does not require replacing the principal date with a retrieval/file date or creating a duplicate report. If the earlier webpage date is judged insufficient to establish first report publication, record that exact unresolved date meaning in the source/evidence note instead of treating it as a settled earlier report release.
