## One substantive classification concern

`draft-gmp-annex-22-artificial-intelligence-2025` needs an explicit record-level adjudication before admission. Its JSON calls the six-page document a principal consultation deliverable because it is independently linked, titled and line-numbered. Those properties establish independent citability, but do not by themselves establish that an annex is a principal record. The data dictionary reserves attachment for independently citable annexes; the historical specification requires an evidenced parent relationship for attachments. Here PDF page 1 explicitly identifies Annex 22, page 2 says it supplies additional guidance to Annex 11, and the consultation frames it as a new annex of EudraLex Volume 4.

This is a modelling concern, not a defect in the recovered dates, relevance or drafting credits. The official consultation also clearly treats the new annex as a separate draft among three consultation texts, which is the strongest evidence for a standalone-proposal interpretation. The parent should decide that interpretation explicitly and consistently with existing section/attachment rules. If the independently citable text is treated as an attachment or separate draft version of the parent collection, the corresponding published parent relationship must be resolved; merely choosing principal to avoid that requirement would weaken the gate. I did not silently change the recommendation or create a parent record.
